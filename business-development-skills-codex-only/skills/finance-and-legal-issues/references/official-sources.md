# Austrian sources and verification

Use these as discovery starting points, **not frozen facts**. Open the relevant current page for the student's year, activity, legal form, and circumstances. Prefer statute/competent agency over a slide or generic blog. Include direct links beside applied claims. If official sources conflict, check effective dates and scope and leave the disputed treatment unresolved if necessary.

## Registration, legal form, taxation, insurance
- WKO Oberösterreich: https://www.wko.at/ooe — find current Gründerservice contacts, regional formation advice, founder events, and the applicable legal-form comparison.
- WKO trade-registration overview: https://www.wko.at/gruendung/gewerbeanmeldung-ueberblick
- WKO requirements and registration: https://www.wko.at/gruendung/gewerbeanmeldung
- WKO Upper Austria 2026 founder guide (dated teaching context, verify changing figures): https://www.wko.at/ooe/gruendung/leitfaden-gruendung.pdf
- USP registration page: https://www.usp.gv.at/gruendung/EAP/gewerbeanmeldung.html — verify any redirect. Use https://www.usp.gv.at/ for current tax, bookkeeping, company formation, and registration guidance.
- GISA: discover the official registration service through USP/WKO; do not send students to a guessed form endpoint.
- BMF: https://www.bmf.gv.at/ — official tax guidance, current-year tariffs, VAT, and self-employed guidance.
- Income-tax tariff discovery page: https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/arbeitnehmerveranlagung/steuertarif-steuerabsetzbetraege/steuertarif-steuerabsetzbetraege.html — select the correct year and distinguish marginal bands from effective tax.
- USP small-business VAT rule: https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-zur-umsatzsteuer/weitere-steuertatbestaende-und-befreiungen/kleinunternehmen.html — check turnover definition, eligible years, exclusions, options, and cross-border scope directly.
- SVS: https://www.svs.at/ — current insurance duties, thresholds, contribution bases, multiple insurance, and exceptions. Old examples on otherwise current pages may still use historical values.
- WKO multiple insurance: https://www.wko.at/sozialversicherung/mehrfachversicherung
- RIS: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/ — current Austrian legislation where necessary.
- Student aid: https://www.stipendium.at/; family allowance via BMF and https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/. Research only benefits the student actually receives or asks about.
- Competent city/district authority via official Linz/Land Oberösterreich pages; registration authority depends on actual location/activity.

## Funding, events, pitching
- tech2b: https://www.tech2b.at/
- WKO Oberösterreich / Gründerservice via https://www.wko.at/ooe
- hub,ert: https://www.hub-ert.net/ — verify current operation and linked organisers.
- aws: https://www.aws.at/ ; FFG: https://www.ffg.at/
- Vienna support/events: official WKO Wien and https://wirtschaftsagentur.at/; follow to event organiser pages.
- JKU entrepreneurship/support events: https://www.jku.at/ — discover the current unit and event listings.

Do not embed fixed future event dates in this skill. Search after the student gives availability. Verify date/year, timezone, city, intended audience, attendee versus pitching eligibility, deadlines, price, and cancellation/update notices. If no dates match, give a verified ongoing consultation/programme and say it is not a confirmed event. Link a general calendar only as a calendar, never as proof of a particular event.

## IP and university inventions
- Austrian Patent Office: https://www.patentamt.at/
- Protection overview: https://www.patentamt.at/schutz-anmelden
- Patent and pre-disclosure novelty guidance: https://www.patentamt.at/schutz-anmelden/erfindungen-anmelden/patent-national
- Searches for patents, marks and designs: https://www.patentamt.at/recherche and https://seeip.patentamt.at/
- JKU IPR / Patent Scouts: https://www.jku.at/ipr-management/ — verify current contacts and process before recommending a named person or applying a university rule.
- EPO/EUIPO official sources may supplement Austrian sources for European rights; do not apply US patent or copyright registration rules to Austria.

Distinguish copyright arising under applicable law from registrable rights; do not state all IP requires registration. Do not confuse inventorship/authorship, share ownership, employment, and transferred/licensed rights. Keep confidential technical details out of public search queries.
