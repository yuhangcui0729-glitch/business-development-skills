## Self-contained conceptual scope
- Financial plan: revenue minus operating expenses gives an operating result; profit differs from cash availability. Separate startup assets/deposits, monthly fixed and per-sale variable costs, and financing flows. Use the calculation guide for contribution and break-even.
- Funding: equity shares risk/ownership; debt adds repayment obligations; grants, guarantees and incubator support have scheme-specific eligibility, timing and conditions.
- Registration: activity authorisation, business legal form, tax registration, accounting, social insurance and any sector permits are distinct questions. Legal form affects ownership, liability, governance, financing and taxation; never choose by tax rate alone.
- Taxes and insurance: VAT is distinct from personal income/corporate tax. Turnover, business profit and personal taxable income differ. Social insurance obligations and benefits may interact with employment/studying, so verify the actual situation and year.
- IP: assess inventions, designs, branding, original works, know-how and third-party rights. Clarify creator, owner/licensee, employer/university context, confidentiality, new team members and disclosure timing. The applicable right depends on the asset, not simply whether an idea sounds original.
- Employment/insolvency: flag relevant contracts, payroll costs, payment difficulties and duties proportionately, referring to verified current sources rather than giving a compulsory extra lecture.
